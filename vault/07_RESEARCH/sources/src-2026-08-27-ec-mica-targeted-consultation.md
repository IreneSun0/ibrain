## 委员会明说想要什么 (原文口径)

> 「a clear and detailed narrative, demonstrated by data (where possible), concrete examples, legal references and qualitative evidence. Specific suggestions for solutions to address issues raised are welcome.」
> 「The Commission services welcome **specificity and brevity**.」

## §4.4 问题原文与作答格式

| Q | 原文 (节选) | 格式 |
|---|---|---|
| 69 | "Do prediction markets present opportunities or risks for EU consumer and investors? If so, please elaborate." | 是/否 **+ 自由文本** |
| 70 | "Should prediction markets, where they are DLT enabled and facilitated through smart contracts be governed by MiFID or MiCA?" | **二选一**: MiFID / MiCA |
| 71 | "What substantive requirements should be considered for service providers that provide prediction market services?" | **纯自由文本** |
| 72 | "Should perpetual futures on crypto-assets and services towards such perpetual futures be governed by MiCA or MiFID." | MiFID / MiCA / No |
| 73 | "What substantive requirements should be considered for perpetual futures...?" | **纯自由文本** |

⚠ **Q70 是强制二选一, 没有「都不是 / 归各国博彩法」选项**; 想表达第三种立场只能写进 Q71 的自由文本。

## §4.4 节前言 (原文摘录)

> 「One of the most marked developments in crypto markets since MiCA was adopted is the emergence of DLT-based prediction markets and the volume of trading activity in perpetual futures on crypto-assets.」
